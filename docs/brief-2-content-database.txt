## 3. AI FIELD AGENT

Create a dedicated AI assistant called:

**THE FIELD AGENT**
*Your AI companion for understanding the invisible city.*

It should have five capabilities:

**IDENTIFY**
Users upload a photograph and ask what they are looking at. The AI analyses the image, suggests an identification, explains visual clues and links to relevant stories.

**RESEARCH**
Users ask questions about objects, buildings, street details, infrastructure and urban history. The agent retrieves relevant information from the curated database and verified research sources.

**NEARBY DISCOVERIES**
With permission, the agent finds nearby mapped discoveries and suggests short exploration routes.

**CONNECTIONS**
The agent recommends related stories based on chapter, subcategory, function, appearance, history and location.

**PERSONALISED EXPLORATION**
The agent generates observation challenges to encourage users to notice overlooked urban details.

The AI must prioritise the curated content database. It should cite sources, distinguish book-derived information from external research and communicate uncertainty.
